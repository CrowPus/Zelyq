# The Engineering Research Book

## Version 1 traceability matrix

Version: 1.0

Status: Reviewed

Last review: 2026-08-25

This matrix traces the final evaluation framework to reviewed research. It is a navigation aid; chapter claim–evidence records remain authoritative for evidence, confidence, and prohibited inference.

## Confidence inheritance rule

A synthesis or engineering claim cannot receive stronger confidence than its weakest material research premise unless new, appropriate evidence is added and reviewed. Missing evidence cannot be supplied by product preference, benchmark popularity, or implementation success on unrelated tasks.
