## Central question

> What do software engineers actually do, and what distinguishes reliable engineering judgment?

Part II examines observable activities, representations, inquiries, decisions, and feedback practices. It does not equate reliability with job title, tenure, confidence, process conformity, or code volume. Its seven chapters move from the portfolio of engineering work through understanding, framing, diagnosis, design, risk, and expertise.
