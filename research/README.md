# The Engineering Research Book

Version 1 is a reviewed, evidence-backed foundation for reasoning about trustworthy AI-assisted software engineering. It studies engineering work, team knowledge, AI capabilities and limitations, justified reliance, and evaluation. It does not define or authorize a product implementation.

## Read the book

1. Start with [Why this book exists](00-front-matter/04-why-this-book-exists.md).
2. Use the [Table of contents](TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md) to follow Parts I–VI.
3. Consult the [research methodology](00-front-matter/06-research-methodology.md) and [evidence standard](00-front-matter/07-evidence-standard.md) when evaluating a finding.
4. Use the [traceability matrix](TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md) to connect synthesis claims to reviewed chapters and evidence records.

## Research record

Version 1 contains forty reviewed research chapters. Every chapter has a corresponding `artifacts/erb-XX-YY/` directory containing its proposal, protocol, search log, source inventory, screening record, evidence table, analysis notes, claim–evidence records, and review record. Additional pilot and correction records are retained where they form part of the audit history.

## Using the research for engineering

Read [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) before applying a finding to engineering work. Research informs a decision; it is not itself a product requirement or permission to write code. The separate [engineering application register](07-zelyq-engineering/REGISTER.md) stays empty until a decision passes the governance process — it holds its first entry, ZED-0001, as of 2026-08-25.
