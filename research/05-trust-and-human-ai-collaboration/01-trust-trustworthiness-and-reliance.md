## Primary research question

> How should trust, trustworthiness, reliance, and calibration be distinguished in AI-assisted software engineering?

## Findings

### Trust is an attitude; reliance is behavior

A person may feel confident in a system but reject its advice, or rely on it under policy or workload while distrusting it. Attitude surveys cannot substitute for observed acceptance, verification, delegation, or intervention.

### Trustworthiness is evidence-relative system quality

For this book, trustworthiness means possessing and demonstrating properties that justify bounded reliance: validity, reliability, security, accountability, transparency where useful, controllability, and recovery in the intended context.

### Calibration concerns conditional reliance

Appropriate reliance means using correct assistance and rejecting or escalating incorrect or out-of-scope assistance under stated conditions. High acceptance, satisfaction, adoption, or trust is not the target.

### Reliance belongs to a sociotechnical arrangement

Users rely on a configured model, tools, organization, controls, evidence, and responsible people—not an abstract model alone. Institutional arrangements can be trustworthy even where interpersonal trust in software is conceptually disputed.

## Evaluation consequence

A responsible evaluation must observe both correct use and correct refusal or intervention. It must define the responsibility, consequence, model/harness, user expertise, available evidence, authority, timing, workload, controls, and recovery path. Adoption, satisfaction, approval rate, explanation presence, or a nominal human-in-the-loop are not sufficient outcomes.

## Limitations and update condition

This structured review is non-exhaustive. Much direct human evidence uses bounded decision tasks rather than sustained software projects, and legal or organizational contexts differ. Material new meta-analysis, replicated engineering-field evidence, model/harness change, or a new attack/control class requires review.

## Research boundary

This chapter studies justified reliance generally. It does not inspect Zelyq, assign a product role, select an architecture, or authorize code.
