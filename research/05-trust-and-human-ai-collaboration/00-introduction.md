## Central question

> What makes reliance on an AI engineering system justified rather than merely convenient?

Part V treats reliance as a conditional behavior that must be supported by evidence. It separates user attitudes, actual acceptance or delegation, demonstrated system properties, human–AI team outcomes, and institutional accountability.
