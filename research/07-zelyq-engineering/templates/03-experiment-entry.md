| Status | Meaning | Work allowed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | Question and initial boundary are recorded | No |

## Research question

State the question the experiment is intended to help answer.

## Existing evidence and uncertainty

Explain why existing evidence is insufficient and what uncertainty the experiment can reduce.

## Project context and verified facts

Record the system, environment, data, users, constraints, and observation dates relevant to the experiment. Separate verified facts from assumptions and unknowns.

## Hypothesis or exploratory objective

State the hypothesis without presenting it as an expected result, or identify the work as exploratory.

## Experimental boundary

Define the environment, systems, data, participants, time period, permitted implementation, isolation, and prohibited uses.

### Stop and return to review when

Define events that terminate execution, require rollback, create a security or privacy concern, exceed the data or environment boundary, or invalidate the method.

## Method and measures

Describe procedure, comparison, data collection, analysis, success and failure criteria, and threats to validity.

## Ethics, privacy, security, and operational safeguards

Record required reviews, consent, authorization, data handling, monitoring, termination, recovery, and cleanup.

## Reviewer declarations

Each reviewer records their role, relevant competence, relationship or conflict, independence limits, materials reviewed, and review date.

## Outcome, closure, and adoption boundary

Record results, failures, limitations, cleanup, retained artifacts, and whether success or failure criteria were met. State whether the experiment produces a research artifact, requires further research, or motivates a later engineering entry. No result authorizes production adoption without a separate approved Zelyq engineering entry.
