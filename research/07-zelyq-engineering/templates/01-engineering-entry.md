## Problem statement

Describe the observed problem, who or what is affected, current behavior, consequence, and evidence that the problem exists. Do not define the problem as the absence of a preferred feature.

## Research coverage

For each relevant finding, record:

| Finding | Scope and relevance | Confidence | Limitations | Current enough? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Not assessed | — | — | — | — |

State whether the evidence is sufficient for an application decision. If not, change status to `Research required` and link the resulting proposal or experiment.

## Project context and verified facts

Record the Zelyq-specific users, behavior, architecture, constraints, measurements, obligations, and other verified facts that affect the decision. Cite the source or observation date for changeable facts.

## Assumptions and unknowns

Separate assumptions and unresolved questions from verified facts and research findings.

## Options considered

Include no change, non-code responses, narrower interventions, technical alternatives, and further research where relevant.

| No change | | | | |

## Proposed decision

State the selected response and the reasoning that connects research, project context, and engineering judgment. Do not describe the decision as a research finding.
