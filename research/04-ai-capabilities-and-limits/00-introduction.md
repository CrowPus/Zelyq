## Central question

> Which parts of engineering work can AI systems perform reliably, and under what conditions do they fail?

Part IV evaluates configured systems rather than model names in isolation. Every changeable claim is bounded by model/system version, harness, context, tools, task, verifier, environment, attempts, and observation date when those details are available.
