## Central question

> How do teams coordinate engineering work and preserve understanding as software evolves?

Part III examines capabilities that emerge through dependencies among people, artifacts, processes, and organizations. It distinguishes collective capability from the sum of individual skills and treats memory as a maintained ability to retrieve and apply relevant knowledge, not a document count.
