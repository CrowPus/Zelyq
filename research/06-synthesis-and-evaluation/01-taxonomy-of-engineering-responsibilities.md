## Primary research question

> How can the responsibilities identified across the book be organized without erasing their contextual and collective nature?

## Findings

### Purpose and consequence responsibility

Identify the need, affected parties, desired and harmful outcomes, constraints, legitimacy, system boundary, and conditions under which work should not proceed.

### Epistemic responsibility

Acquire task-relevant context; distinguish observation, inference, assumption, uncertainty, and disagreement; form models; make predictions; and revise them after evidence.

### Decision responsibility

Generate or recognize alternatives, expose tradeoffs and temporal effects, record rationale and authority, decide within scope, and preserve triggers for reconsideration.

### Construction and change responsibility

Produce bounded changes, maintain interfaces and invariants, manage dependencies, preserve maintainability, and connect implementation to its authorized purpose.

### Verification and operational responsibility

Select claim-matched checks, validate oracles, observe real outcomes, detect failure, contain consequences, recover state, and learn after deployment.

### Coordination and memory responsibility

Route dependency information, review consequential work, maintain ownership and escalation, transfer tacit knowledge, preserve evidence and rationale, and support onboarding.

### Governance and rights responsibility

Enforce authorization, security, privacy, accountability, auditability, contestability, and redress throughout the lifecycle.

## Approved synthesis findings

1. **Engineering responsibility spans seven connected families — Moderate:** trace to ERB-01-S, ERB-02-S, ERB-03-S; do not infer the taxonomy is ontologically final.
2. **Responsibility is allocated across actors and artifacts, not inherently to one engineer — Moderate:** trace to ERB-01-02, ERB-03-S; do not infer collective responsibility means no individual accountability.
3. **Each responsibility requires a purpose-specific evidence object — Moderate:** trace to ERB-02-S, ERB-04-06, ERB-05-02; do not infer one artifact per responsibility is mandatory.
4. **Governance and rights are lifecycle responsibilities, not deployment add-ons — Moderate:** trace to ERB-01-03, ERB-05-06; do not infer every context has identical legal duties.

See the [claim–evidence records](../artifacts/erb-06-01/claim-evidence-records.md).

## Use boundary

These findings define research-grounded evaluation structure. They are not a product specification, architecture, model selection, backlog, or authorization to implement. A separate engineering agent must apply them to verified project context through the governance process.

## Limitations

This chapter inherits every material upstream limitation and adds synthesis risk: categories may simplify interacting responsibilities, and operationalizations require validation in the intended context. No synthesis confidence exceeds its weakest material premise.
