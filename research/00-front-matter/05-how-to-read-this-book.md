# The Engineering Research Book

# How to Use This Book

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Introduction

The Engineering Research Book is not intended to be read like a traditional book.

It is intended to be used as a living body of engineering research.

Some readers will read it from beginning to end.

Others may only read a single chapter.

Researchers may spend weeks contributing to one section.

Engineers may reference it before implementing a feature.

AI agents may analyze multiple chapters before making design recommendations.

Every reader has a different purpose.

This document explains how the book should be used.

---

# The Purpose Of This Book

The purpose of this book is not simply to provide information.

Its purpose is to improve engineering decision making.

Every chapter should help answer one or more important engineering questions.

Every chapter should increase understanding.

The objective is not to memorize the book.

The objective is to apply its knowledge.

---

# Who Should Read This Book?

This book was written for multiple audiences.

Each audience should approach it differently.

---

# Researchers

Researchers investigate problems.

Your responsibility is to improve knowledge.

Do not begin with solutions.

Begin with questions.

Collect evidence.

Challenge assumptions.

Document your reasoning.

Improve existing research before creating new research whenever possible.

Your goal is not to be correct.

Your goal is to discover what is correct.

---

# Contributors

Contributors improve the book.

Before making changes, read the existing research.

Understand why previous conclusions were reached.

Do not replace existing work without first understanding it.

Support significant changes with evidence.

Every contribution should improve clarity, accuracy, or understanding.

---

# Reviewers

Reviewers protect the quality of the research.

Do not review writing style first.

Review reasoning first.

Ask questions.

Challenge unsupported conclusions.

Request additional evidence when necessary.

Identify missing perspectives.

Ensure that conclusions follow logically from the available evidence.

---

# Engineers

Engineers should use this book before designing systems.

Do not ask:

"What feature should we build?"

Instead ask:

"What problem has already been researched?"

Engineering decisions should begin with research.

Implementation should follow understanding.

Whenever possible, trace engineering decisions back to specific chapters.

---

# Product Designers

Design decisions should originate from developer problems identified within the research.

Design should solve verified problems.

Avoid designing around assumptions.

---

# Product Managers

Use this book to prioritize work.

Problems supported by stronger evidence should generally receive higher priority than problems supported only by assumptions.

Roadmaps should be informed by research.

Not popularity.

---

# AI Agents

AI systems participating in Zelyq development should treat this book as the primary source of engineering philosophy.

Before proposing new features,

AI agents should identify:

Which problem is being solved?

Where was the problem researched?

What evidence supports the problem?

What conclusions currently exist?

If the research is missing,

the AI should recommend further investigation rather than inventing unsupported conclusions.

---

# Founders

Founders should use this book to protect long-term vision.

Features change.

Technology changes.

Markets change.

Principles should remain stable.

When difficult product decisions arise,

return to the research.

Ask whether the proposed direction remains consistent with the philosophy established in this book.

---

# Reading Order

Readers do not always need to begin with Chapter One.

Different goals require different paths.

Examples:

A new contributor should begin with the Front Matter and Contribution Guide.

An engineer implementing a feature should begin with the relevant research chapter.

A reviewer should begin with the evidence supporting the chapter under review.

A founder may revisit the philosophical chapters before evaluating major strategic decisions.

Choose the reading path that matches your purpose.

---

# Reading Research

Do not treat conclusions as permanent truth.

Research evolves.

Evidence changes.

Conclusions improve.

Remain open to better explanations.

Challenge respectfully.

Support your arguments.

Improve the work.

---

# Using Research In Product Decisions

Every major engineering decision should answer four questions.

What problem are we solving?

Where was that problem researched?

What evidence supports it?

How will we know the solution worked?

If these questions cannot be answered,

the decision should be reconsidered.

---

# Updating Research

Research should evolve continuously.

When new evidence appears,

the appropriate chapter should be updated.

Do not create duplicate chapters for the same topic.

Improve existing knowledge whenever possible.

Research should accumulate rather than fragment.

---

# Cross References

Readers are encouraged to follow related chapters.

Engineering problems rarely exist in isolation.

Architecture influences developer experience.

Developer experience influences adoption.

Adoption influences product direction.

Product direction influences engineering priorities.

The research should therefore be viewed as an interconnected system rather than isolated documents.

---

# A Living Book

Unlike traditional books,

this project is expected to change.

Some chapters will be rewritten.

Some conclusions will change.

Some hypotheses will be disproven.

This is a sign of progress.

The purpose of research is not to defend previous conclusions.

The purpose is to improve understanding.

---

# Final Thought

The value of this book does not come from the number of pages it contains.

Its value comes from the quality of the questions it asks, the evidence it gathers, and the decisions it helps people make.

Use this book as a guide.

Question it.

Improve it.

Help it grow.

In doing so, you also help improve the future of software engineering.
