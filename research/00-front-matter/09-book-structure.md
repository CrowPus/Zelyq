Version: 0.1

# Purpose

This document defines the complete structure of **The Engineering Research Book**.

It exists to ensure that every contributor, researcher, engineer, AI agent, and future maintainer understands how knowledge inside this project is organized.

This document should be considered the architectural blueprint of the research.

No chapter should be created unless it belongs within this structure.

If the structure needs to change, the architecture should be updated first before adding new content.

---

# Why This Book Exists

The Engineering Research Book is not documentation.

It is not a user manual.

It is not marketing material.

It is not a design document.

It is a continuously evolving body of research whose purpose is to answer one question:

> **How do we build the world's most trusted AI Software Engineer?**

Every chapter exists because answering that question requires research.

---

# Research Philosophy

The research follows five principles.

## Principle 1

Research before implementation.

Ideas are investigated before they are built.

---

## Principle 2

Evidence before opinion.

Evidence always has higher priority than assumptions.

---

## Principle 3

Understanding before optimization.

A problem must first be understood before attempting to solve it.

---

## Principle 4

Challenge every conclusion.

Every conclusion is open to criticism when supported by better evidence.

---

## Principle 5

The book is never finished.

Knowledge evolves.

The research evolves with it.

---

# Research Categories

Every statement should belong to one category.

## Fact

Supported by strong evidence.

---

## Observation

Observed repeatedly but may require further validation.

---

## Hypothesis

A proposed explanation that requires research.

---

## Opinion

Personal interpretation.

Opinions should be clearly identified.

---

# Evidence Levels

Evidence should also include confidence.

Level A

Multiple independent sources.

Very high confidence.

---

Level B

Several reliable sources.

High confidence.

---

Level C

Limited evidence.

Needs additional validation.

---

Level D

Single source.

Treat carefully.

---

Level E

Speculation.

Never use as the basis for product decisions.

---

# Contribution Rules

Every contributor agrees to the following.

Challenge ideas.

Not people.

Question assumptions.

Support claims.

Cite evidence.

Accept criticism.

Admit uncertainty.

Change opinions when evidence changes.

Protect research quality.

Protect intellectual honesty.

---

# Relationship With Zelyq

The Engineering Research Book is not separate from Zelyq.

It is one of the core products of Zelyq.

Every major feature should trace its origin back to research contained in this book.

If no research supports a feature,

the feature should be questioned.

Research drives design.

Design drives implementation.

Implementation produces feedback.

Feedback creates new research.

The cycle continues.

---

# Closing Statement

The purpose of this book is not to prove that Zelyq is correct.

The purpose is to discover what is true about software engineering.

Only by understanding the discipline can we build an AI system worthy of becoming a trusted engineering partner.

This book should remain open, evidence-driven, and intellectually honest for as long as Zelyq exists.
