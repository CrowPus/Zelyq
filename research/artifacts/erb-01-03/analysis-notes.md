# The Engineering Research Book

## ERB-01-03 analysis notes

Record version: 0.1

Status: Gate B reviewed

## Cross-source synthesis

The evidence distinguishes at least four uncertainty forms: incomplete or unproven knowledge represented as assumptions; uncertainty about future value and time; ambiguity in language and interpretation; and uncertainty about how architectural choices affect multiple qualities. Constraints enter through time, resources, business criticality and urgency, technical risk, stakeholder priorities, and quality-attribute interactions.

Tradeoff visibility is relational rather than merely numerical. The same debt item changes priority when represented through business processes (S003); uncertainty language changes meaning across speakers and readers (S004); and scenario/utility-tree methods require stakeholders to define qualities and priorities (S009/S010). No included evidence establishes that a more explicit artifact guarantees a better decision.

## Alternative explanations

- Stated temporal preferences may reflect scenario framing, uncertainty interpretation, task scale, or elicitation model rather than stable discounting behavior.
- Changed technical-debt rankings may reflect researcher facilitation or selected participants rather than the representation alone.
- Uncertainty expressions may serve politeness or authority management rather than epistemic disclosure.
- ATAM case benefits may reflect skilled facilitation, stakeholder attention, or project maturity.
- Published assumption-management literature may overrepresent formal methods and report positive lessons selectively.

## Sensitivity checks

- Removing S009/S010 leaves evidence that perspectives and time horizons affect decisions, but removes the detailed quality-tradeoff artifact model.
- Removing S001/S002 removes the replicated temporal-choice effect but leaves assumptions, business priorities, and architecture tradeoffs.
- Removing S003/S004 weakens the finding that tradeoffs are negotiated through representation and communication.
- No finding depends on inaccessible S006–S008.

## Coverage limits

Evidence is thin for legal, ethical, accessibility, security-specific, operational, maintenance, and ecological constraints; for measured decision quality; and for prospective comparison of tradeoff methods. These remain research gaps, not assumed absences.
