## ERB-03-03 research proposal

Status: Approved and executed

## Primary research question

> How are consequential technical decisions made, challenged, recorded, and revised across a project's lifetime?

Purpose: identify bounded mechanisms, collective responsibilities, and observable evidence. Mode: structured non-exhaustive evidence synthesis and conceptual analysis. Excluded: universal process prescriptions, AI product requirements, Zelyq decisions, and application code.
