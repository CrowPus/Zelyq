# ERB-03-03 analysis notes

## Mechanism synthesis

- **Architecture is a history of consequential commitments:** Structures and technologies embody decisions about qualities, constraints, interfaces, and organizational capabilities. Recovering only the final diagram omits why rejected options and assumptions mattered.
- **Decision records need a minimum causal spine:** A durable record connects context and stakeholders to drivers, considered alternatives, chosen response, evidence, assumptions, consequences, status, and triggers for reconsideration. A template field without evidence does not preserve rationale.
- **Challenge must occur before and after commitment:** Scenarios, reviews, prototypes, and dissent can expose an Achilles heel before adoption. Operational and maintenance evidence can expose it later. Authority should resolve action while preserving unresolved disagreement and uncertainty.
- **Records must evolve without rewriting history:** A superseding decision should retain the old basis, new evidence, migration consequences, and effective boundary. Editing a past record to appear prescient destroys learning and traceability.

## Sensitivity and unresolved evidence

Removing self-report reduces confidence but leaves direct observational, repository, case, or experimental support where listed. Removing single-organization sources narrows claims to mechanisms rather than prevalence. Correlations are not interpreted as causes, practice accounts are not effectiveness tests, and artifact completion is not treated as an outcome. Longitudinal causal comparisons and diverse organizational contexts remain sparse.
