# ERB-06-06 analysis notes

## Derived structure

- **Defensible conclusion: engineering exceeds code production:** Across Parts I–III, software engineering is continuing sociotechnical responsibility for purpose, evidence, uncertainty, change, operation, coordination, and learning.
- **Defensible conclusion: AI capability is real but bounded:** As of August 25, 2026, configured systems demonstrate multiple useful engineering-task capabilities, but evidence does not establish reliable autonomous lifecycle responsibility.
- **Defensible conclusion: reliance must be conditional:** Task evidence, calibrated uncertainty, enforced authorization, meaningful oversight, recovery, accountability, and continuing validation are required in proportion to consequence.
- **Non-generalization: benchmark to field:** Public task success does not directly estimate production reliability, organizational fit, user impact, security, maintenance, or longitudinal learning.
- **Non-generalization: one domain or population:** Evidence overrepresents public repositories, Python, technology organizations, bounded online experiments, English interaction, and accessible published outcomes.
- **Open agenda: causal field evidence:** Priority questions include longitudinal developer/team outcomes, skill and knowledge change, incident rates, verification load, organizational incentives, small and non-Western settings, accessibility, privacy, energy/cost, and rare high-consequence failure.
- **Open agenda: evaluation science:** Needed work includes contamination-resistant living tasks, validated oracles, realistic adversarial environments, consequence-aware measures, correlated-failure analysis, memory update/deletion, oversight capacity, and post-deployment recovery.
- **Open agenda: governance and affected parties:** Research must examine who defines purposes and thresholds, how power and consent shape data and delegation, how people contest outcomes, and how accountability survives vendor and model change.

## Sensitivity

Removing Part IV prevents current AI capability conclusions; removing Part V prevents justified-reliance criteria; removing Parts I–III collapses engineering responsibilities into AI benchmark constructs. Therefore no part can be substituted for another. Upstream Moderate–Low findings remain no stronger here. The synthesis does not resolve missing field, cultural, accessibility, legal, environmental, or high-consequence evidence.
