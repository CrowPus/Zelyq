# ERB-03-06 analysis notes

## Mechanism synthesis

- **Ownership combines knowledge, duty, and authority:** Contribution counts can approximate familiarity but do not show current understanding, operational responsibility, or power to act. Ownership must name the object, obligations, backup, and escalation boundary.
- **Concentration and diffusion have different risks:** Concentrated knowledge can speed decisions while increasing succession and review dependence. Diffusion can broaden resilience while obscuring accountability. Repository correlations vary by process and metric.
- **Technical debt is a decision relation, not a smell total:** The useful metaphor links a present compromise to future contingent cost, benefit, affected work, evidence, and repayment options. Principal and interest are multi-faceted and do not map reliably to one static-analysis number.
- **Maintenance health is demonstrated through change outcomes:** Lead time, regressions, recovery, comprehension effort, dependency currency, and repeated work can expose change friction. Refactoring activity or debt repayment claims require outcome evidence.

## Sensitivity and unresolved evidence

Removing self-report reduces confidence but leaves direct observational, repository, case, or experimental support where listed. Removing single-organization sources narrows claims to mechanisms rather than prevalence. Correlations are not interpreted as causes, practice accounts are not effectiveness tests, and artifact completion is not treated as an outcome. Longitudinal causal comparisons and diverse organizational contexts remain sparse.
