# ERB-06-04 synthesis protocol

Status: Executed

Date: 2026-08-25

Traverse approved findings and claim–evidence records in dependencies: ERB-01-S, ERB-02-S, ERB-03-S, ERB-04-S, ERB-05-S. Extract responsibility/capability/criterion/scenario/measure/gap as applicable, upstream claim ID or chapter, confidence, population/configuration boundary, contrary evidence, and prohibited inference. Reject any synthesis item without upstream support or label it explicitly as a proposed operationalization.

Gate A checks dependency completeness and traceability. Gate B checks inferential distance, preservation of limitations, internal consistency, non-product boundary, and whether evaluation criteria remain observable and falsifiable.
