# ERB-03-04 research protocol

Status: Executed

Date: 2026-08-25

Search the chapter concepts with software engineering, empirical, field study, repository study, experiment, review, limitation, replication, and contrary result. Include sources with inspectable method or an explicitly bounded first-party practice role. Extract population, setting, unit, mechanism, measure, observation, causal status, limitation, and prohibited inference. Seek method diversity and disagreement. Do not pool unlike outcomes or infer prevalence from cases.

Gate A requires direct evidence plus qualifying or contrary coverage. Gate B reviews every finding’s scope, confidence, alternative explanations, and separation from Zelyq. This protocol supports a structured review, not a comprehensive systematic review.
