# ERB-06-01 analysis notes

## Derived structure

- **Purpose and consequence responsibility:** Identify the need, affected parties, desired and harmful outcomes, constraints, legitimacy, system boundary, and conditions under which work should not proceed.
- **Epistemic responsibility:** Acquire task-relevant context; distinguish observation, inference, assumption, uncertainty, and disagreement; form models; make predictions; and revise them after evidence.
- **Decision responsibility:** Generate or recognize alternatives, expose tradeoffs and temporal effects, record rationale and authority, decide within scope, and preserve triggers for reconsideration.
- **Construction and change responsibility:** Produce bounded changes, maintain interfaces and invariants, manage dependencies, preserve maintainability, and connect implementation to its authorized purpose.
- **Verification and operational responsibility:** Select claim-matched checks, validate oracles, observe real outcomes, detect failure, contain consequences, recover state, and learn after deployment.
- **Coordination and memory responsibility:** Route dependency information, review consequential work, maintain ownership and escalation, transfer tacit knowledge, preserve evidence and rationale, and support onboarding.
- **Governance and rights responsibility:** Enforce authorization, security, privacy, accountability, auditability, contestability, and redress throughout the lifecycle.
