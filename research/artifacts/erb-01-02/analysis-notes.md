## Alternative explanations retained

- Technical-only: code structure, architecture, interface design, or tooling may explain an outcome without a material social mechanism.
- Process-only: workflow design or task decomposition may account for coordination demands.
- Organizational: authority, incentives, staffing, and resources may dominate artifact relationships.
- Ecological/environmental: market, regulatory, vendor, or infrastructure coupling may dominate local design.
- Measurement: observed relationships or null results may arise from proxy validity, outcome choice, aggregation level, or missing communication channels.
