## How to read this inventory

Candidate status means only that a source appeared relevant enough, at title/abstract level, to warrant full-text screening. It does not mean the source is included, appraised, or permitted to support a finding. The "Operational-boundary read (title/abstract only)" column records a provisional, unverified judgment against the protocol's five-part test — at least one technical element, at least one person/team/organization/process/policy/institution/user-group/environmental element, a relationship or dependency, a connected behavior/adaptation/decision/constraint/outcome, and an adequate system boundary and level of analysis. Every "likely yes" in that column must be confirmed, not assumed, at full-text screening.

## Inventory rule

Candidate status means only that a source appears relevant enough for full-text screening. It does not mean that the source is included, appraised, or permitted to support a finding.

For each retained candidate, sufficient provenance to identify the source, version, and stable location is recorded above where available. Several candidates (S001, S003, S013, S037) have incomplete provenance and are explicitly flagged for confirmation before proceeding. Full-text availability, access restrictions, source dependencies, and current disposition must remain visible as screening advances.

## Identifier convention

Identifiers are assigned sequentially as `ERB-01-02-S001`, `ERB-01-02-S002`, and so on, in the grouped order presented above (foundational theory and definitions; Conway's-law/congruence empirical studies; coordination and ownership empirical studies; CSCW; incident and resilience mechanism studies; open-source governance; alternative theoretical frameworks). Grouping is for readability only; it does not imply a priority ranking. No identifier is reused after exclusion.
