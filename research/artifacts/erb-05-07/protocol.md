# ERB-05-07 research protocol

Status: Executed

Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-25

Search trust in automation, appropriate reliance, over- and under-reliance, explanation, oversight, control, intervention, reversibility, recovery, privacy, security, accountability, complementarity, experiments, meta-analysis, null effects, and contrary outcomes as applicable. Extract population, task, assistance condition, AI correctness manipulation, human/AI baselines, behavioral and attitudinal measures, consequence, method, and limitation.

Gate A requires empirical human evidence plus conceptual or governance boundaries and contrary/mixed outcomes. Gate B rejects adoption, satisfaction, explanation presence, human presence, and process completion as standalone proof of justified reliance. This is a structured review, not a comprehensive systematic review.
