# ERB-03-01 analysis notes

## Mechanism synthesis

- **Coordination demand follows interdependence:** Code, task, schedule, data, and decision dependencies create needs for awareness and agreement. Team boundaries or meeting calendars do not remove those dependencies.
- **Communication is one coordination mechanism:** Direct conversation can resolve ambiguity and unforeseen coupling, while issue trackers, interfaces, ownership, tests, plans, and automation coordinate asynchronously. The appropriate mix depends on latency, consequence, and persistence needs.
- **More communication is not the target:** Communication has interruption, interpretation, and scaling costs. The target is that affected actors receive usable information before dependent action, with a persistent record when later accountability or recovery requires it.
- **Missing links are material failure modes:** Failures include an unrecognized dependency, wrong recipient, late message, vocabulary mismatch, unrecorded decision, unavailable expert, and action without acknowledgement. Message volume cannot show that coordination succeeded.

## Sensitivity and unresolved evidence

Removing self-report reduces confidence but leaves direct observational, repository, case, or experimental support where listed. Removing single-organization sources narrows claims to mechanisms rather than prevalence. Correlations are not interpreted as causes, practice accounts are not effectiveness tests, and artifact completion is not treated as an outcome. Longitudinal causal comparisons and diverse organizational contexts remain sparse.
