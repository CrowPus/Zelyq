# ERB-01-04 analysis notes

## Synthesis decisions

- “Understanding” is treated as a family of task-conditioned capabilities, not an internal state inferred from one proxy.
- Code structure, behavior, intent, history, dependencies, ownership, tests, and environment remain separate information classes.
- S004’s time estimate is retained as a result in its two-company setting, never as a universal constant.
- S006 supplies a mechanism for bounded tracing tasks, not proof that all comprehension difficulty is memory load.
- S007 is preserved because a theoretically plausible representation did not produce a significant aggregate performance improvement in its small experiment.

## Sensitivity checks

Removing S004 eliminates the quantitative time statement but not the question, distribution, or cognitive findings. Removing student-containing studies leaves S001’s industrial arm and S002/S004 workplace evidence. Removing self-report leaves direct observation, instrumentation, and task outcomes. No approved finding depends on the deferred S003 source.

## Gaps

Evidence remains weak for long-lived safety-critical systems, distributed and multilingual teams, non-code operational knowledge, accessibility, and longitudinal retention. The reviewed measures do not yield a validated single comprehension construct.
