# ERB-01-04 evidence table

| Evidence | Observation | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S001 | participants asked questions ranging from locating focus points to relationships and behavior across subgraphs | understanding is question- and task-relative | an exhaustive universal taxonomy |
| S002 | 17 observed developers obtained information from tools, artifacts, and coworkers during normal work | understanding is socially and artifact distributed | prevalence outside the observed organization |
| S004 | classified activity from 78 professionals across 3,148 hours; reported substantial comprehension activity and use of browsers/documents | comprehension is material work extending beyond IDE code views | a universal percentage or causal effect of tools |
| S005 | 44 participants read test suites; prior project knowledge and experience related to selected measures | context and specialized knowledge can affect test comprehension | that tenure guarantees understanding |
| S006 | experiments linked program-state retention, interference, and tracing strategy to errors | local tracing is constrained by working memory | a complete account of system-level understanding |
| S007 | 20-person experiment found no significant overall performance advantage for a spatial canvas; time allocation differed | plausible aids require outcome testing | equivalence of all interfaces or absence of any benefit |
