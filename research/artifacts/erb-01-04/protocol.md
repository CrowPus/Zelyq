## Design

Use a structured, non-exhaustive synthesis. Treat understanding as task-relative: extract the task, setting, information sought, source used, observable measure, and permitted inference for every study.

## Search concepts

Combine `program comprehension`, `software understanding`, `information needs`, `maintenance`, `change task`, `mental model`, `working memory`, `test comprehension`, `field study`, `professional developer`, `experiment`, `limitation`, and `null result`.

## Eligibility and appraisal

Include complete primary studies with inspectable methods and a material software-understanding question. Appraise participant population, task realism, system scale, observation method, construct validity, comparison, statistical power, and generalizability. Do not convert time spent, navigation behavior, confidence, or a task score into a universal measure of “understanding.” Preserve null results.

## Synthesis and stopping rule

Organize evidence by questions asked, information location, cognitive constraint, task context, and measurement. Stop after the evidence includes workplace observation, task experiments, specialized test comprehension, cognitive mechanism evidence, and a contrary tool result, and a supplementary pass produces no new central category. Gate A checks source coverage; Gate B checks every claim boundary and prohibited inference.
