## Primary research question

> Why do software systems become difficult to understand, and which forms of understanding does engineering work require?

## Purpose and scope

This review identifies the information engineers seek, where it resides, the cognitive and organizational conditions that make it difficult to recover, and the limits of common comprehension measures. It includes observational and field studies of professional work, controlled comprehension studies, and contrary results. It excludes AI capability claims, product requirements, and any inspection of Zelyq.

## Supporting questions

1. Which questions arise during change, diagnosis, design, testing, and coordination?
2. Which kinds of knowledge cannot be recovered from source text alone?
3. How do task, prior knowledge, tools, people, and working-memory constraints affect comprehension?
4. What do time, correctness, navigation, recall, and self-report measures actually establish?
5. Which proposed aids fail to improve measured performance?
