# ERB-01-05 research protocol

Status: Executed

Date: 2026-08-25

Use structured, non-exhaustive synthesis of foundational arguments, longitudinal repository studies, ecosystem dependency studies, controlled reliability evidence, and documented accident investigation. Extract mechanism, system boundary, time scale, evidence type, outcome, alternative explanation, and prohibited inference. Search `software evolution`, `software aging`, `longitudinal`, `breaking change`, `dependency`, `maintenance`, `accident`, `common-mode failure`, and contrary tests of evolution laws. Preserve disagreement and do not treat a case as a prevalence estimate.

Gate A requires evidence at codebase, ecosystem, runtime/design, and sociotechnical-accident levels. Gate B requires each conclusion to distinguish observation, mechanism, and prescription.
