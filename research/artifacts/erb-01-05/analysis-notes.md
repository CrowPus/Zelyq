# ERB-01-05 analysis notes

The synthesis distinguishes five mechanisms: environmental mismatch, internal deterioration during change, dependency propagation, latent/runtime activation, and failed organizational control or learning. “Degradation” is not assumed to be monotonic. Successful change can restore fit and improve structure; change can also introduce new faults.

Sensitivity checks: removing the foundational S001 still leaves longitudinal change and dependency evidence, but weakens the aging vocabulary. Removing the Therac-25 case leaves no detailed accident-response mechanism. Removing repository studies leaves no longitudinal test of universal evolution claims. Findings therefore retain different confidence by mechanism and do not count sources as votes.

Open gaps include decommissioning, recovery and resilience outcomes, security-driven degradation, hosted-service drift, user adaptation, accessibility, and causal comparisons of maintenance strategies.
