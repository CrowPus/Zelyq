## ERB-01-05 research proposal

Status: Approved and executed

Prepared: 2026-08-25

## Primary research question

> Through which mechanisms do software systems remain effective, degrade, or fail as their environments and requirements change?

This research separates loss of environmental fit, change-induced internal deterioration, dependency failure, runtime aging, and accident causation. It examines maintenance as continued adaptation rather than proof of permanent correctness. It excludes product-specific engineering and code.
