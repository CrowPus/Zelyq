# ERB-02-03 research protocol

Status: Executed

Date: 2026-08-25

Search the chapter title, primary-question concepts, empirical studies, professional practice, systematic review, experiment, limitations, and null or contradictory findings. Include complete primary studies and inspectable syntheses with direct relevance. Extract setting, sample, task, construct, method, observation, limitation, permitted use, and prohibited inference. Appraise self-report, task realism, construct validity, causal status, independence, and generalizability.

Synthesize mechanisms and observable practices rather than source counts. Gate A requires at least one direct empirical source plus methodological or contrary coverage. Gate B checks every claim boundary. This is a structured review, not an exhaustive systematic review.
