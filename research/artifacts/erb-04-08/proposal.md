## Primary research question

> To what extent do existing evaluations measure capabilities that matter in real software-engineering work?

Mode: structured, non-exhaustive synthesis of dated artifact evaluations, benchmark papers, audits, and relevant human-engineering evidence. Product claims and leaderboard values are not assumed current without dated records. The chapter evaluates general capability constructs and does not inspect or specify Zelyq.
