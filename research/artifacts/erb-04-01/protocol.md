# ERB-04-01 research protocol

Status: Executed

Date and evidence cutoff: 2026-08-25

Search benchmark creators, official repositories, peer-reviewed or inspectable evaluations, independent audits, security tests, null findings, and human comparison evidence. Extract exact model/system version when a result depends on it; harness, tools, prompts, task version, environment, attempts, scoring, observation date, and limitations. Prefer construct and failure-mechanism findings that survive model turnover. Do not transfer one benchmark score to a different engineering responsibility.

Gate A requires creator evidence and material limitation or audit evidence. Gate B checks changeability, version fit, construct validity, causal status, contamination, and separation of model from system/harness capability. This is not an exhaustive systematic review.
