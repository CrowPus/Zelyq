## Amendment A001 — Relevance-adapted extraction headings

Original procedure: the protocol's data-extraction block listed a fixed sequence of fields, including separate definitions of software engineering and programming and a related-or-dependent-sources field.

Change: evidence entries used relevance-adapted headings for different source types. Normative and historical sources received definition-and-scope fields; empirical sources received responsibility, outcome, method, result, limitation, appraisal, permitted-use, prohibited-inference, and candidate-claim fields as applicable. An inapplicable field was not repeated merely to state that the source did not address it. Material dependencies are recorded in the relevant appraisal or joint-synthesis requirement.

Reason: the evidence base combines normative frameworks, historical reports, observational studies, surveys, repository studies, replication work, and meta-analysis. Repeating identical fields for sources that cannot answer them would add empty structure without improving traceability.

Date and research stage: 2026-08-25; documented retrospectively at Gate A after the independent reviewer identified that the implemented schema differed from the literal protocol template.

Information available when changed: source types and relevance became known during full-text extraction. No findings or confidence ratings had been drafted.

Likely effect on coverage, bias, or findings: the adapted headings improve readability and preserve method-specific detail, but inconsistent field visibility could allow an unaddressed concept to go unnoticed. Gate A identified one such cross-source gap: no included source operationally defines programming. The gap is now explicit in the chapter and review record.

Reviewer decision: the AI-assisted reviewer recommended formal documentation under OBJ-07. The author accepted and recorded the amendment. Dee Empire ratified the correction through the Gate A decision on 2026-08-25.
