## Meaning of approval

Approval authorizes the recorded search, screening, appraisal, extraction, and synthesis process to begin. It does not approve a conclusion or presume that software engineering and programming are distinct in every context.

## Conditions retained from the protocol

- Search explicitly for overlapping definitions and criticism of the distinction.
- Separate occupational titles from observed activities and responsibilities.
- Do not treat standards or professional frameworks as empirical proof of actual practice.
- Do not use abstracts, search snippets, or AI summaries as evidence for substantive claims.
- Record full-text exclusions and protocol amendments.
- Do not draft findings until relevant sources have passed screening and appraisal.
