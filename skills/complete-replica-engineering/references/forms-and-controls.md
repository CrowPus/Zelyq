# Forms and Controls

## Visual states
Match:
- resting;
- hover;
- focus;
- filled;
- invalid;
- disabled;
- read-only;
- loading/submitting;
- success.

## Native behavior

Prefer real inputs/buttons/selects or established accessible primitives rather than drawing fake controls.

## Geometry

Record:
- control height;
- internal padding;
- label/helper gap;
- icon position;
- border/radius;
- focus ring.

## Validation
If the reference shows validation:
- trigger timing;
- message placement;
- icon/status;
- field retention;
- submit state.

Do not fake the look while changing fundamental behavior unless scope is explicitly visual-only.
