# Profile: E-commerce

## Priorities
- findability;
- product evaluation;
- trust;
- price/availability clarity;
- low-friction checkout;
- recovery.

## Apply domain research carefully
Baymard research is useful for search, product lists/pages, cart, checkout and account flows.

Do not invent urgency/scarcity.

## Key states
- unavailable/out of stock;
- variant unavailable;
- delivery uncertainty;
- payment pending/failure;
- promo errors;
- cart mutation;
- guest/account paths.
