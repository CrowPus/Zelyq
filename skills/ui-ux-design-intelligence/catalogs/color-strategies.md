# Color Strategies

## Neutral + single action accent
Best for: productivity, enterprise, tools.
Strength: clear hierarchy.
Risk: bland without typography/brand craft.

## Brand-led semantic
Best for: established products with strong brand palette.
Rule: separate decorative brand usage from status semantics.

## Warm natural
Best for: wellness, hospitality, lifestyle.
Risk: muted colors may fail contrast.

## High-contrast editorial
Best for: publishing, creative, cultural.
Risk: fatigue if every element competes.

## Dark technical
Best for: developer/media/monitoring contexts.
Risk: glow/color overload and poor light-environment usability.

## Monochrome + status
Best for: highly dense tools.
Strength: status colors retain meaning.
Risk: hierarchy depends heavily on type/spacing.

## Data palette
Choose based on:
- categorical;
- sequential;
- diverging.

Do not use decorative brand palette blindly for charts.
