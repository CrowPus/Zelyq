# Typography Strategies

## System/product sans
Best for dense apps and cross-platform consistency.

## Humanist sans
Good for approachable product experiences and long UI copy.

## Grotesk / geometric sans
Strong brand/marketing presence; verify small-size legibility.

## Serif display + sans body
Good for editorial/luxury/culture; keep serif to appropriate roles.

## Serif-led editorial
Useful for reading/content brands; less suitable for dense controls unless carefully designed.

## Monospace accent
Use for code, identifiers, logs, technical values.
Do not use monospace as a generic "developer aesthetic" for all body copy.

## Variable fonts
Can reduce family complexity and enable optical/weight adaptation, but test browser/platform support and file cost.

## Pairing rule
Pair by complementary function, not by "two fonts look cool."

One family with strong role differentiation is often better than forced pairing.
