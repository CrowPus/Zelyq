# UI/UX Design Intelligence Evaluation Rubric

Score 0–5.

1. **Product understanding** — design responds to actual user/business goal.
2. **Information architecture** — content/navigation/grouping are coherent.
3. **Interaction design** — actions, feedback, cancellation, recovery.
4. **Visual hierarchy** — priority is immediately perceivable.
5. **Design system coherence** — type/color/spacing/shape/motion form a system.
6. **Accessibility** — design accounts for WCAG/platform accessibility before implementation.
7. **Responsive/adaptive quality** — information and interaction adapt, not merely shrink.
8. **Content design** — labels, errors, empty states, tone.
9. **Platform/domain fit** — conventions and product archetype are respected.
10. **Restraint/craft** — avoids gratuitous trends; alignment/rhythm/details are intentional.

## Critical failures

Automatic fail if:
- an existing product is unnecessarily redesigned during a feature task;
- an essential action is inaccessible or gesture-only without justified alternative;
- critical text/control contrast knowingly fails without remediation;
- destructive/high-cost workflow offers no reasonable recovery/confirmation;
- fake testimonials, metrics, scarcity, or claims are invented;
- mobile is a materially broken scaled desktop;
- the design claims usability because it "looks modern";
- a chart materially misrepresents data;
- important states are omitted and the design is declared finished.

## Passing target

- total ≥ 40/50;
- no critical failure;
- product understanding, interaction, and accessibility each ≥ 3.

Flagship/high-craft work: target 45+/50.
