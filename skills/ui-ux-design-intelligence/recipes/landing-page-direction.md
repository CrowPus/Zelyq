# Recipe: Landing Page Direction

## Start with content

Write:
- audience;
- problem;
- differentiated promise;
- proof;
- CTA.

## Storyboard
A common structure:

1. positioning;
2. product evidence/demo;
3. key outcomes;
4. proof/trust;
5. objections/details;
6. CTA.

Change order based on audience awareness.

## Visual direction
Choose one dominant art direction and one secondary accent.

## Product visuals
Prefer real:
- screenshots;
- product diagrams;
- demos;
- customer artifacts.

Do not use abstract 3D decoration if the actual product remains unexplained.

## Conversion review
- Is promise specific?
- Is proof credible?
- Is CTA proportionate?
- Are pricing/terms transparent?
