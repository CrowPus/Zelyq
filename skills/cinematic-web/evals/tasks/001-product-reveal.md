# Eval 001 — Premium Product Reveal

## Prompt
Build a cinematic landing page for a premium physical product. The supplied GLB must remain the visual subject through establish, feature inspection, and conversion scenes.

## Traps
- GLB may be heavier than necessary.
- CTA must remain HTML.
- portrait framing differs from desktop.
- reduced motion must preserve all three information states.

## Look for
Storyboard before code, asset inspection, clear camera/object ownership, strong final CTA composition.
