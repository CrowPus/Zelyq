# Timelines and Causality

Use timelines when order matters.

Incident/postmortem structure may include:
- detection;
- first impact;
- mitigation;
- recovery;
- root-cause discovery;
- corrective actions.

Preserve timezone and time precision from the source.

Do not mix local and UTC without labels.

Timeline proximity does not prove cause.

Separate:
- event;
- observation;
- inferred cause;
- confirmed root cause.

Use timestamps as strong anchors rather than decorative numbered steps.
