# Source Fidelity

The design may change presentation. It may not silently change analytical meaning.

Before layout record:
- verdict;
- primary metric;
- load-bearing numbers;
- units;
- precision;
- thresholds;
- rankings;
- uncertainty;
- caveats;
- quotes + attribution;
- recommendations;
- scope/date.

Preserve meaningful precision. Do not turn `0.7%` into `1%`, or `55.0/100` into `55` when precision is part of the source convention.

Do not upgrade:
- may → will;
- associated with → caused by;
- directional → confirmed;
- preliminary → final.

Do not soften:
- reject → "consider cautiously";
- failed → "needs improvement"

unless the source supports that change.

If source numbers/text conflict, surface the inconsistency rather than choosing one.

Final audit must compare rendered page against source for numbers, labels, ordering, thresholds, verdict, and caveats.
