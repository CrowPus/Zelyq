# Profile: Research / Evidence Synthesis

## Reader need
Understand question, evidence quality, agreement/conflict, uncertainty, and conclusion.

## Structure
1. research question;
2. summary conclusion;
3. evidence map;
4. themes/findings;
5. contradictory evidence;
6. limitations;
7. implications.

## Evidence hierarchy
Visually distinguish:
- measured/primary;
- external secondary evidence;
- inference;
- hypothesis.

## Avoid
- flattening all sources into equal credibility;
- "consensus" claims unsupported by source;
- turning qualitative themes into fake percentages.
